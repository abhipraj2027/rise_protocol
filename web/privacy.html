Rise Protocol — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Short version: Rise Protocol collects nothing. There is no account, no server, and no network connection at all — everything it stores lives only on your device, in the app's own local database, until you delete the app.

What this app is

Rise Protocol is an alarm clock app for Android. It lets you create alarms, wakes you up, and requires you to complete a short "mission" (such as solving a math problem) before an alarm can be dismissed.

Data we collect

None. Specifically:

  • No account, sign-up, or personal information is ever requested.
  • No data is transmitted off your device — the app makes no network requests of its own.
  • Alarms, labels, and settings are stored only in a local database and local preferences on your device (via SQLite and Android's SharedPreferences), never uploaded anywhere.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Permissions the app requests, and why

  • Exact alarms / Alarms & reminders — required so the OS wakes the app at the precise time you set, instead of a delayed approximation.
  • Notifications — used to deliver the full-screen ringing alert when an alarm fires.
  • Full-screen intent (Android 14+) — lets the ringing screen take over the display, including over a locked screen, instead of showing only a small notification.
  • Vibration, wake lock, foreground service — used while an alarm is actively ringing, to keep the device awake and play the alarm sound reliably.
  • Run at startup (boot-completed) — used only to re-schedule your existing alarms after the phone restarts. It does not start any network activity.
  • Battery optimisation exemption — optional; some phone manufacturers aggressively stop background apps, which can silently break alarms. This exemption is requested only if you choose to grant it during setup.

Data retention and deletion

All app data lives on your device only. Uninstalling Rise Protocol deletes it. Since nothing is collected anywhere else, there is nothing for us to retain or for you to separately request the deletion of.

Children's privacy

Rise Protocol is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age — see "Data we collect" above.
